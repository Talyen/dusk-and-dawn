DUSK & DAWN — ENEMY MECHANICS CATALOGUE v0.1
110 independently authored action pools · working proposal
All health and effect values require playtesting. No resistances. No separate theme tags.
Hush: Suppress the enemy’s labeled trait through its next action. Skip a non-attack ability; an attack keeps its base hits. Advance the pattern normally.
Leech: Heal for the attack’s final damage after bonuses and Weaken, before Ward. Count Ward absorption and overkill. Healing stops at maximum HP.
