Dusk & Dawn · continued status revision

Halving statuses. Fewer target prompts.

Current directions are applied across the rules, 496 card faces and 110 enemies. Timing, rounding and values are provisional. Bless, Curse, Freeze, Cleanse and Purge now have approved directions. Numerical scaling and Freeze thaw remain open; see the linked current comparison.

Current scaling comparison, control decisions and prompt audit

Applied rule contract

  • Absorb incoming damage before HP. At the start of its holder’s turn, halve remaining Ward, rounding down. Remove it at 0.
  • At player turn end, deal Fire damage equal to Burn, then halve Burn, rounding down. Remove it at 0. Applying Burn keeps the higher value; it does not add.
  • At player turn end, deal Decay damage equal to Poison, then halve Poison, rounding down. Remove it at 0. Applying Poison keeps the higher value; it does not add.
  • Reveal the top card of your library for the rest of this player turn. If the top changes, reveal the new top.
  • Give the stated cards a Keep marker. They survive this turn’s discard on their current faces. “Keep this card” returns the resolving source to your hand, marked, when its effect finishes.
  • Turn the stated cards to their other faces. Whole-hand, self, top-of-pile and random targets are explicit and require no selection prompt.

Provisional timing: player Ward halves before refill and draw. Clear Scry when End Turn begins; discard unkept cards; tick player Burn, player Poison, enemy Burn, then enemy Poison. Halve each damaging status immediately after its hit and remove 0. If the enemy survives, halve its remaining Ward before its action, even if Hushed. Stop immediately on defeat. Poison and Burn apply by maximum value, separately. Rounding down prevents immortal 1-point counters.

Balance and gameplay implications

  • Ward banking makes spare defense useful across turns and can make quiet enemy turns more defensive. Repeated gains may support stalls: 10 Ward every turn with no damage approaches 19 from zero after gain. Values remain unchanged pending playtests; test healing plus Ward and shield-stripping counters.
  • Burn loses damage faster: Burn 3 changes from 3+2+1=6 to 3+1=4; Burn 4 becomes 4+2+1=7. Poison uses the same curve with Decay damage. Existing Burn amounts are preserved; the six new Poison faces use provisional values. Both counters coexist, can be absorbed by Ward, and use max application rather than additive stacking. They deliberately share their math; their current differences are their independent counters, damage labels and specific cures or feeding interactions.
  • Damage-over-time instances are not direct attacks. Neither Burn nor Poison is modified by weakness, Bless, Curse, Reflect or Leech. Fire replaces the damage label only; the card named Flame and all artwork filenames retain their identities.
  • Scry trades top-card filtering for turn-long information. Scry followed by Draw reveals the next top too, supporting informed draw, ordering and facing decisions without a prompt. A pure Scry face may be weak at 1 mana; test it before adding unrelated rider effects.
  • Self-Keep preserves a particular known physical card, including its opposite face for later Flip. Whole-hand Keep can preserve too much and restrict fresh draws: seven kept cards allow only one new card under the cap. It never grants a free replay; every play still costs 1 mana.
  • Self-Flip plus self-Keep gives Cocoon, Flower Bud and other transformation subjects a direct change of form. The original played description finishes even after its source flips. Random targets reduce prompts but reduce precision; sample only after payment, without replacement, and preview the pool rather than a secret result.
  • Pottery Workshop, Glassblower and Tarnished Mirror now transform the visible top discarded card. End-turn discard order follows hand order rather than asking for an ordering choice; bespoke effects that explicitly say “in any order” retain that deliberate choice.
  • The Mould Harpist spreads spores; Saffron Spider and Velvet Widow deliver venom. The Janitor removes both damaging residues. Poison 2 totals 3 before prevention; Poison 3 totals 4. These are thematic proposals with different pressure, not tested encounter balance. The Plague Doctor explicitly treats Poison; most other recovery remains unchanged.
  • Bless and Curse are now stacking whole-attack modifiers; Mark and Weaken are retired. Cleanse and Purge print one random kind or all. Scaling and Freeze thaw remain unresolved; see the current comparison and prompt audit.

Bless · current direction and open details

Status

approved stacking buff; numerical scaling open

Current direction

Affects every hit of the owner’s next direct attack; applications add and all stacks are spent on that attack. The catalogue shows a labeled provisional flat model.

Open decision

Flat +1 per stack versus percentage scaling; no final unit is chosen.

Curse · current direction and open details

Status

approved stacking debuff; numerical scaling open

Current direction

Affects every hit of the holder’s next direct attack; applications add and all stacks are spent. It replaces Weaken; Mark is also removed.

Open decision

Flat −1 per stack versus percentage scaling; compare actual attack values before deciding.

Freeze · current direction and open details

Status

approved enemy action control; thaw open

Current direction

If still active, skip the next whole enemy action, advance the pattern and clear Freeze. It does not stack or queue turns.

Open decision

Whether direct attacks or Fire thaw it is unresolved. Current conditional previews do not choose either rule.

Purge · current direction and open details

Status

approved direction and quantities

Current direction

Remove one random beneficial status or all from the enemy, as printed. One clears a whole kind, regardless of stack count.

Scope

Ward, Reflect and Bless; not printed traits, action patterns or later grants.

Cleanse · current direction and open details

Status

approved direction and quantities

Current direction

Remove one random harmful status or all from self, as printed. One clears a whole kind; random is uniform by eligible kind.

Scope

Burn, Poison, Curse, Hush, Freeze and visible draw/mana penalties. It does not heal HP or recover cards.

Current removal scope

  • beneficial combat statuses: Ward, Reflect, Bless
  • harmful combat statuses: Burn, Poison, Curse, Hush, Freeze, next-normal-draw penalty, next-mana-refill penalty
  • other state: Keep markers belong to individual cards, Scry is player-turn information, face orientation and queued facing changes are not automatically harmful, HP, library/discard/Banish zones, printed traits and action patterns are not removed
  • principle: One random kind or all is printed explicitly. Random is not weighted by counter size. This is mechanical removal scope, not a thematic tag system.
