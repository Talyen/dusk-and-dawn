Working design · effects v0.2

Dusk & Dawn
Card Effect Catalogue

248 physical cards, 496 distinct faces. Each pair is one two-sided card. Every face costs 1 mana to play. Themes and effects are proposed for playtesting, not final balance.

Reading a face: spend 1 mana to play it, then resolve its sentences in order. Flip changes the remaining hand. Ward absorbs damage; Burn does not stack: use the higher of its current value or the amount applied. At the end of your turn it deals that much Flame damage, then decreases by one. Enemy Ward absorbs Burn damage. Banish removes the whole physical card for this battle.

The ten themes also name damage types: Radiance, Shadow, Growth, Decay, Binding, Release, Flame, Frost, Memory and Oblivion. A slash means one attack has both types; it matches either weakness but gains the bonus only once. Burn deals Flame damage. “Lose HP” is a direct payment, not damage, and bypasses Ward. Numbers and card availability are provisional.

Rules reference

Play and targets: Check for 1 mana and any required targets before paying. Mana refills to 3 each turn and cannot exceed 3; a mana-gain card gains at most 1 and banishes itself. Cards cannot be copied, and banished cards cannot be recovered in the first build. The played card leaves your hand while resolving, then goes to discard unless banished. Resolve its sentences in order. Optional actions do nothing when unavailable. Your hand holds at most eight cards.

Flip: Only cards still in your hand turn over. Each card keeps its face through discard and reshuffle; all reset to light at the next fight.

Ward and Burn: Your Ward expires at the start of your next turn. Enemy Ward expires immediately before its next action, after the preceding Burn tick. Burn does not stack: keep the higher value; at the end of your turn it deals that much Flame damage, then decreases by one. Enemy Ward absorbs Burn damage.

Weakness and healing: If a direct card attack has the enemy’s weakness among its damage types, add 2 damage once before Ward, including attacks that ignore Ward. The bonus does not apply to Burn or utility. Healing restores the printed amount, up to maximum HP, regardless of Ward or the attack’s outcome.

Banish: A banished card, including both faces, is removed for the fight and returns for the next one. If the draw pile is empty when drawing or looking at its top card, shuffle the discard pile first.
