# Artwork and gameplay audit

This is a continuing, explicitly partial audit of the current playable runtime. The entries below were individually viewed as the actual WebP artwork, then compared with the printed runtime effects and their handlers at `e6caa9c`. Notes reflect thematic judgment, not proof of balance or independent verification of every interaction. Earlier catalogue reviews have broader thumbnail coverage; this list records only the images inspected in this pass.

## Cards: five pairs, ten faces

| Pair                         | Artwork seen and current behavior                                                                                                               | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Candle & Extinguished Candle | A burning candle refunds its mana, applies Burn and Banishes itself; its smoking, unlit face turns the remaining hand dark and Banishes itself. | The fire and loss of light give both effects a direct visual cue. The mana refund makes the Burn a one-use tempo play.                                                                                                  |
| Lantern & Shroud             | A bright lantern Scry-and-draws; a veiled figure Keeps the other cards in hand.                                                                 | The lantern reveals what lies ahead; the shroud preserves what is held. | Mirror & Shattered Mirror    | The intact mirror grants one-use Reflect and Banishes; the fractured mirror deals two Release hits.                                             | Reflection and shards map directly to the effects. | Paved Road & Deer Trail      | A bright paved route Scry-and-draws; a moonlit winding deer path draws and then Flips the other hand, including the card drawn.                 | Both show moving ahead; the trail's change of direction is a looser cue for Flip. ## Enemies: three entries

| Enemy                | Artwork seen and current behavior                                                                                                                                                 | Assessment                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lantern Eater        | A skeletal maw reaches for a lit lantern. Its trait consumes remaining Burn after the tick and heals 3; the base attack still happens under Hush.                                 | Devouring the flame for recovery fits the image. A small Burn can disappear during the tick before the trait checks it.     |
| The Painted Raven    | A giant multicolored raven stands above a painter. Its Mimic action copies the last direct hit's final value that turn, clamped to 3–8, or uses 3 without a hit.                  | Imitating a stroke fits the painted subject. The last hit of a multi-hit card is the relevant value, so card order matters. |
| The Mirror-Eyed Swan | A white swan has multiple reflective eyes and a mirrored pool. Its displayed Mirror Wing reflects the first hit of the next direct card attack; Hush suppresses this immediately. | The reflection is visible in the artwork, and a multi-hit attack can still land later hits.                                 |

The runtime review used the live `cards` and `simpleEnemies` definitions and the resolution in `run.ts` and `direct-attack.ts`, including existing targeted tests. `tests/lib/thematic-content-interactions.test.ts` adds controlled checks for Candle versus the Lantern Eater, both Mirror faces versus the Swan, and attack order versus the Raven. A browser regression plays both Mirror faces against the Swan, reloads the saved fight and resolves the next action. These are constructed encounters, not natural full-run or balance studies. No card or enemy effects were changed in this pass; no claim is made about entries outside this list.

No card or enemy effects were changed in this pass; the Weaver was retained after checking the draw-slot consequence rather than judging its top-of-library movement in isolation.

## Third pass: class kits and encounters

At `142e8af`, the ten additional card-face WebPs and three enemy WebPs below were individually inspected against their current runtime text and handlers. These are the actual assets, not an assertion that the rest of the catalogue has been reviewed. Earlier playtest notes also discuss some of these class cards.

| Pair                    | Artwork and behavior                                                                                               | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Seraph & Fallen Seraph  | A many-eyed, radiant winged being deals 7 Radiance; its damaged, fallen counterpart costs 2 HP and deals 9 Shadow. | The fallen figure's torn wings support the health sacrifice and darker attack. Its higher base damage offers a riskier alternative.                                                                                                |
| Feather & Chain         | An illuminated feather draws two cards; bound figures on a long chain apply Curse 2.                               | The chain's restraint is direct; drawing cards from a feather is much more abstract. The Seraph kit uses it for options instead of another attack, and there is no clearly superior thematic replacement that preserves that role. |
| Censer & Plague Brazier | A glowing incense burner applies Curse 2; green, skull-shaped fumes apply 3 Poison.                                | Poison follows the sickly fumes immediately. The censer's suppression is less visually specific, though ritual smoke plausibly impedes the enemy; its dark face offers sustained damage instead.                                   |
| Oracle & Deceiver       | A haloed seer Scry-and-draws; a masked puppeteer randomly Flips a held card.                                       | Foresight and manipulation fit the figures. The random Flip avoids an additional target-selection prompt.                                                                                                                          |
| Benediction & Curse     | Light descends onto a figure for Bless 3 and self-Banish; a figure bound in red ribbons applies Curse 2.           | Empowerment and restraint are legible in the images, with a one-use offensive setup versus repeatable defense.                                                                                                                     |

| Enemy            | Artwork and behavior                                                                                                                                 | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The Brass Cicada | A large golden insect makes Radiance attacks; its rattling wings add three small hits to its second action.                                          | The bright body and vibrating wings suit the attack and repeated hits. Flat Curse currently reduces each hit, which makes Chain especially effective; changing that rule remains a separate open decision. |
| The Blue Jackal  | A blue jackal stands beneath a moon; its Shadow attacks include a howl that grants it Bless after attacking.                                         | The moonlit hunt supports Shadow and the attack-building howl, though the numerical Bless is not literally visible.                                                                                        |
| Hollow King      | A crowned, hollow figure reaches over the player; its Shadow attacks apply Curse on Hollow Clutch, and Empty Decree Banishes the library's top card. | The empty grasp and removal evoke loss and depletion. The exact library movement is an abstract interpretation of the artwork.                                                                             |

`tests/lib/class-art-interactions.test.ts` compares Feather with Chain against the Cicada, Censer with Plague Brazier against the Jackal, and Benediction followed by Seraph against the Hollow King's Radiance weakness. In these controlled states, the Seraph loses 5 HP after Feather's support Ward versus 1 after Chain under the current flat Curse rule; the Witch loses 3 HP with Censer versus 5 with Plague Brazier, while Poison deals 3 to the Jackal. A browser scenario also plays Benediction, reloads, and resolves Seraph and the King's attack. These checks do not establish balance or verify every face independently.

Chain, Censer, Curse and previously reviewed Ivy all currently apply Curse 2; their opposite faces and class kits differ. Their shared visible outcome and Feather's abstract draw remain candidates for future playtesting, but no replacement here was clearly more thematic without changing gameplay or introducing extra complexity. No card or enemy effect was changed in this pass. Bless/Curse scaling remains unchanged and unresolved.
