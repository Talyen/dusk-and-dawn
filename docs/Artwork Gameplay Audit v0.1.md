# Artwork and gameplay audit

This is a continuing, explicitly partial audit of the current playable runtime. The entries below were individually viewed as the actual WebP artwork, then compared with the printed runtime effects and their handlers at `e6caa9c`. Notes reflect thematic judgment, not proof of balance or independent verification of every interaction. Earlier catalogue reviews have broader thumbnail coverage; this list records only the images inspected in this pass.

## Cards: five pairs, ten faces

| Pair                         | Artwork seen and current behavior                                                                                                               | Assessment                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Candle & Extinguished Candle | A burning candle refunds its mana, applies Burn and Banishes itself; its smoking, unlit face turns the remaining hand dark and Banishes itself. | The fire and loss of light give both effects a direct visual cue. The mana refund makes the Burn a one-use tempo play.                                                                                                  |
| Lantern & Shroud             | A bright lantern Scry-and-draws; a veiled figure Keeps the other cards in hand.                                                                 | The lantern reveals what lies ahead; the shroud preserves what is held. Its light face duplicates Paved Road's printed effect, but its other face supplies a different choice.                                          |
| Mirror & Shattered Mirror    | The intact mirror grants one-use Reflect and Banishes; the fractured mirror deals two Release hits.                                             | Reflection and shards map directly to the effects. The two faces offer defense or damage.                                                                                                                               |
| Wax Seal & Torn Letter       | A large red wax seal gives 7 Ward and Banishes; a visibly torn letter draws two and Banishes.                                                   | The seal-as-protection and tearing-as-consuming are plausible, but the numeric Ward and draw are relatively generic. No alternative was clearly more faithful without adding mechanics or changing balance arbitrarily. |
| Paved Road & Deer Trail      | A bright paved route Scry-and-draws; a moonlit winding deer path draws and then Flips the other hand, including the card drawn.                 | Both show moving ahead; the trail's change of direction is a looser cue for Flip. The light face duplicates Lantern, while the dark faces support different decisions.                                                  |

## Enemies: three entries

| Enemy                | Artwork seen and current behavior                                                                                                                                                 | Assessment                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Lantern Eater        | A skeletal maw reaches for a lit lantern. Its trait consumes remaining Burn after the tick and heals 3; the base attack still happens under Hush.                                 | Devouring the flame for recovery fits the image. A small Burn can disappear during the tick before the trait checks it.     |
| The Painted Raven    | A giant multicolored raven stands above a painter. Its Mimic action copies the last direct hit's final value that turn, clamped to 3–8, or uses 3 without a hit.                  | Imitating a stroke fits the painted subject. The last hit of a multi-hit card is the relevant value, so card order matters. |
| The Mirror-Eyed Swan | A white swan has multiple reflective eyes and a mirrored pool. Its displayed Mirror Wing reflects the first hit of the next direct card attack; Hush suppresses this immediately. | The reflection is visible in the artwork, and a multi-hit attack can still land later hits.                                 |

The runtime review used the live `cards` and `simpleEnemies` definitions and the resolution in `run.ts` and `direct-attack.ts`, including existing targeted tests. `tests/lib/thematic-content-interactions.test.ts` adds controlled checks for Candle versus the Lantern Eater, both Mirror faces versus the Swan, and attack order versus the Raven. A browser regression plays both Mirror faces against the Swan, reloads the saved fight and resolves the next action. These are constructed encounters, not natural full-run or balance studies. No card or enemy effects were changed in this pass; no claim is made about entries outside this list.
