# Card outcome audit v0.1

The 496 reviewed faces are partitioned below by their printed effect. Every face has at least one outcome scenario; this is not a claim that every combination of statuses, zones, enemy traits, random choices, or artwork interpretations has been independently verified.

|   185 | Draw and Scry, fixed discard returns and Banish, self-Keep/Flip, whole-hand changes, and random Flip/Keep; includes empty pools and the hand cap.                                                                             | `tests/lib/card-flow-families.test.ts`                                                                                                                                       |

The machine-readable index at `tests/fixtures/card-outcome-coverage.json` records each pair, face, and its outcome scenario. `tests/lib/card-outcome-manifest.test.ts` checks catalogue and runtime identity, family patterns and counts, and the presence of the referenced test names; it cannot establish that every clause or interaction is asserted.

The random-discard payment scenarios explicitly check each printed payoff and both the payment card and source card destinations. Isolated mutations suppressing Confession healing or Frenzy damage caused the corresponding scenario to fail.

The bespoke set is Imp, Revenant, Sacrifice, Rain, Extinguished Candle, Memory, Marionette, Cartographer, Lost Wanderer, Locust, Wilderness, Night Market, Loaded Dice, Chessboard, Repulsion, Solitude, Low Tide, Chimney Sweep, Dandelion, Sieve, Funnel, Counting House, Termite Palace, Stone Eater, Lantern Snuffer, Path Eraser, Day Moth, Night Moth and Ice Crystal.

Family expectations are derived from the printed descriptions and use explicit zone, face, status, mana and source-disposition assertions. Random-target checks verify eligibility, counts and empty pools, not statistical uniformity. Some tests use controlled snapshots to isolate a rule; browser tests separately cover real interaction and persistence. Future work should sample multi-effect interactions across enemies and real players, especially Leech, Reflect, Hush, Freeze, and card-order comprehension. Bless/Curse scaling and early Freeze thaw remain undecided.

Wax Seal, Torn Letter, Wooden Spool and Loose Thread use the runtime playtest overrides recorded in `src/lib/game/run.ts`; their historical catalogue proposals remain unchanged. The manifest classifies the current printed effects, including overrides, and the dedicated reward-outcome tests check Ward, draw, Keep, Flip and source disposition.
