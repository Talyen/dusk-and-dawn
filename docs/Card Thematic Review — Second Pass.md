# Dusk & Dawn: second card artwork review

All 496 retained artwork images were inspected in 31 contact sheets alongside their effects. The review used thumbnails; it is a thematic assessment, not a full-resolution image-quality or balance assessment. Effects are proposals pending human playtesting. The old attack-reduction keyword Hush was renamed Weaken; Hush now means a one-use silence.
